# Limitations

- Public signing and notarization are prerequisite-bound; unsigned artifacts must not
  be presented as public notarized builds.
- External scanner execution can be represented by deterministic records when a tool
  is unavailable; unavailable tools must remain explicit degraded/block states.
- Advanced cloud or deep red-team claims are not implied unless the relevant
  environment was configured and the applicable gates passed.
- Windows support and unlimited free inference are not V1 claims.
