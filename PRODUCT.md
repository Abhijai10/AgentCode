# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers use AgentCode at their desktop while delegating coding work to an autonomous agent. Their primary job is to start, follow, steer, and verify a coding mission without losing the conversational thread.

## Product Purpose

AgentCode is a desktop workspace for collaborating with an autonomous coding agent. The conversation is the product: activity and technical evidence provide compact, contextual support.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, capable. The product should feel like a trusted technical collaborator, not a dashboard or a social application.

## Anti-references

AI SaaS dashboards, analytics/control-center layouts, generic IDE clones, card-heavy admin panels, and marketing or subscription surfaces.

## Design Principles

- Preserve conversational focus above all supporting information.
- Make autonomy legible through concise activity and evidence, never private reasoning.
- Keep technical detail available on demand in contextual panels.
- Favor calm density, familiar controls, and dependable desktop behavior.
- Keep mock UI state behind typed service boundaries for daemon integration later.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Respect reduced-motion preferences and provide visible focus states.
